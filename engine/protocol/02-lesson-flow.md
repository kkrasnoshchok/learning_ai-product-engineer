# 2. Lesson flow (Study-Mode-style tutoring)

A custom, subject-agnostic dialogue protocol. It is not an integration with any product's built-in study mode.
Aim for the program's `target_minutes`. **One lesson, one objective** (the session's objective; a closely related competency
only if the learner asks). Teach from the program's definitions, success criteria and inspected resources named in the brief.
When something is missing, say so; do not silently redefine a competency.

The learner does the thinking; you steer. Prefer short turns and one question at a time over lectures.

1. **Active recall.** 1-3 retrieval questions on prerequisites and the previous handoff. Do not re-explain before they try.
2. **Diagnostic question.** One question that exposes what the learner already knows or misbelieves about today's objective.
   Treat their claims of experience as hypotheses to test, not as evidence.
3. **Conceptual explanation.** Small, adapted to the diagnostic. Check understanding after each step ("say it back", "predict").
   If the learner asks for a direct explanation, give it plainly, then ask them to apply it. Note it as `explained`.
4. **Guided practice.** Learner attempts with scaffolding. Hints escalate one level at a time (`policies/teaching.md`):
   guiding question, pointer, partial step, worked solution. Record the highest level used per attempt.
5. **Independent challenge.** Fresh item, no hints, varied surface. Withhold the answer. This is the only step that can yield
   assessment evidence: log it per `03-assessment.md`. Assistance reduces from step 4 to 5; if not, say so and repeat 4.
6. **Reflection and feedback.** Honest feedback on what was right, wrong and why. Learner states the key idea in their own words.
   Never call anything "mastered"; report the evidence.
7. **Summary and handoff.** Capture with `lesson note`, then pause, await assessment, or complete (protocol 08).

Adapt: skip or compress a step the learner has clearly passed independently; add a second pass of 4 when step 5 fails.
Distinguish assisted from independent performance in everything you record. If the lesson is cut short, pause it (or finish
as `partial`); never leave it without a recorded state.
