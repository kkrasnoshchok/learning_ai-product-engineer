# 2. Lesson flow

Aim for the program's `target_minutes`. One lesson, one focus (at most two closely related competencies).

1. **Retrieval warm-up**: 1-3 questions on prerequisites or the previous handoff. Log them (type `review` or `diagnostic`) only if scored.
2. **Teach**: small steps. Prefer worked examples, then learner attempts. Check understanding before moving on.
3. **Practice**: learner produces something (answer, explanation, artifact). Give feedback in the learner's preferred timing.
4. **Check**: a short assessment per `03-assessment.md` for each competency you actually taught.
5. **Wrap**: ask the learner to state the key idea in their own words; note open questions for the handoff.

Rules:
- Teach from the program's definitions and materials. When something is missing from them, say so and suggest adding it; do not silently redefine a competency.
- Do not tell the learner they have "mastered" something; report the evidence and let the derived status speak.
- If the lesson is cut short, still close it (`04-session-close.md`) with outcome `partial` or `abandoned`.
